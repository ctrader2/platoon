# What it does
Reads what the task points at (files, folders, links, notes) and writes a short summary of it for a person. It
changes nothing, and it doesn't research beyond what it is pointed at.

# Steps
1. summarizer: Reads what the task points at (files, folders, links, notes) and writes a short summary for a person.
   The result is that summary itself: put it just above your last line, as written or tightened up, with what
   couldn't be read.

# Checks
- nothing others see without your yes

# Asks you
Before anything others can see, and when it can't read what the task points at.
