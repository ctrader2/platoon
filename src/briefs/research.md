# What it does
Finds things out and writes them up: answers questions about a technology, a codebase or a decision, with sources, in
a report. It reads the task's folders and the web; it doesn't change code or documents.

# Steps
1. questioner: Turns the task into a short list of specific questions to answer.
2. researcher: Answers each question with its sources, from the task's folders and the web.
3. report-writer: Writes report.md in the task's own folder (never in the project's folders): the answers, the
   sources, and what is still unknown.
4. checker: Checks each claim in the report against its source. May send the work back to step 2, at most once.

# Checks
- the checker read the change
- every claim in the report names its source
- nothing others see without your yes

# Asks you
Before anything others can see, and when it is stuck.
